ABOUT CCI

Compassion into action.

Creating safer, more supportive environments for young people and making youth mental health part of the conversation in Kenya.

OUR STORY

Why are we still so silent about youth mental health?

When I was younger, I suffered from a severe form of allergic rhinitis. The condition made my eyes watery, my nose runny and eventually swollen. The children around me teased me for my noisy breathing and teary eyes. I had few friends and mostly spent time alone, hidden away and isolated.

At the age of nine, I received surgery that reduced the noisy breathing and allowed me to breathe and sleep better. I could breathe clearly again, my eyes stopped watering, and the bullying finally stopped. I was healed—or so I thought. The surgery addressed my physical health, but I never got to address the negative impact bullying had left on my mental health.

No one talked about mental health. I did not know how to express the pain and discomfort I was dealing with, whether I would be taken seriously, or what I could do about it.

WHY IT MATTERS

Mental health is part of health.

Mental health affects how young people cope with stress, connect with others, learn and participate in their communities. Yet conversations about youth health can overlook the emotional and psychological experiences that shape everyday life.

CCI believes awareness must be paired with practical support. We want young people to have the language, knowledge and supportive environments they need to speak about mental health and seek help when they need it.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Values

Compassion

We listen without judgment and treat every young person's experience with dignity.

Awareness

We believe mental health should be discussed openly, accurately and without shame.

Community

We work with schools and communities to create environments where young people feel supported.

Action

We turn awareness into practical education, advocacy and meaningful community work.

OUR VISION

A future where youth mental wellbeing is at the forefront of adolescent education in Kenya.

CCI envisions a future where every young person, regardless of their circumstances, can access mental health education, supportive communities and the care they need to thrive.
