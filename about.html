OUR STORY

Built on the belief that every child has potential.

Compassionate Children's Initiative began from a simple question: what could happen if young people and communities worked together to make opportunity more accessible?

CCI is a youth-led initiative focused on educational access, mentorship, community support and youth leadership.

Our goal is not simply to provide resources. It is to help build environments where children feel seen, supported and empowered to shape their futures.
