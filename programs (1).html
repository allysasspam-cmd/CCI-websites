OUR WORK

Turning mental health awareness into action.

CCI focuses on youth mental health education, stigma reduction and supportive environments in Kenya.

01

Mental Health Education

Interactive workshops that help young people understand mental wellbeing and build practical skills for everyday challenges.

02

Awareness & Advocacy

School presentations and social media content that encourage open, informed conversations about youth mental health.

03

Supportive Communities

Collaborating with schools and community groups to help young people feel heard and supported.

04

Youth Leadership

Encouraging young people to become advocates who challenge stigma and promote mental wellbeing.
