WHY CCI EXISTS

Opportunity should never depend on where a child is born.

Across communities, talented and curious children are held back by barriers unrelated to their potential. CCI works to bridge those gaps through resources, people and opportunities.

ABOUT US

Compassion is where change begins.

CCI is a youth-led initiative focused on creating greater access to educational resources, mentorship and opportunities for children and underserved communities.

We believe children should not be defined by the resources they have access to. We want to help create environments where curiosity is encouraged, education is valued and every child can imagine a bigger future.

OUR MISSION

Turning compassion into action.

CCI connects children and underserved communities with educational resources, mentorship and meaningful opportunities while encouraging young people to become active contributors to their communities.

WHAT WE DO

Our Work

Our work focuses on creating practical opportunities for children while strengthening the communities around them.

01

Educational Access

Connecting underserved schools and children with books, learning materials, technology and other educational resources.

02

Mentorship

Creating relationships that encourage children to explore interests, develop confidence and see possibilities beyond their current circumstances.

03

Community Initiatives

Working with communities to identify challenges and develop meaningful, locally relevant solutions.

04

Youth Leadership

Empowering young people to become advocates, volunteers, researchers and leaders within their communities.
