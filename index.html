WHY CCI EXISTS

Mental health should never be something a young person has to face alone.

Young people can experience bullying, isolation, stress and other challenges that affect their wellbeing. CCI works to make mental health conversations more visible, accessible and supportive for youth in Kenya.

CCI

OUR STORY

From a personal experience to a community mission.

CCI grew from recognizing that treating a physical problem does not always address the emotional impact left behind. After experiencing bullying as a child, I learned how easily mental health can remain unspoken.

Today, CCI is focused on helping young people understand their mental health, find support and know that their experiences deserve to be taken seriously.

OUR MISSION

Making youth mental health impossible to ignore.

CCI aims to raise awareness, reduce stigma, provide practical mental health education and create safer, more supportive environments for young people in Kenya.

WHAT WE DO

Our Work

We focus on practical ways to help young people recognize, discuss and respond to mental health challenges.

01

Mental Health Education

Interactive workshops that give young people practical knowledge and actionable strategies for navigating everyday mental health challenges.

02

Awareness & Advocacy

Sharing information through school presentations and social media to make conversations about youth mental health more visible.

03

Supportive Communities

Working with schools and communities to encourage environments where young people feel heard, respected and supported.

04

Youth Leadership

Empowering young people to become advocates and leaders who help challenge stigma and promote mental wellbeing.

OUR VISION

A Kenya where youth mental wellbeing is at the forefront of adolescent education.
